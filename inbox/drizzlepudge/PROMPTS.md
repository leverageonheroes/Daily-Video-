## Moral Of The Story TV - MOS-002: The money is gone. Who took it? | The Wedding Fund Part 2

**How to make the clips (phone):**
1. Open Flow with the account named in the section.
2. Choose New project → Frames to Video → Veo 3.1 Lite, 9:16, 1 output.
3. For each clip: long-press the image → Save. Upload it as the start frame, copy the prompt, then Generate.
4. If a clip has an extra person, a changed face or text on screen, generate it again (it's free).
5. Download it, name it with the clip number (`1.mp4`), and upload it to `inbox/drizzlepudge/`.

The film is assembled when all 15 clips are in `inbox/drizzlepudge/` (1.mp4 to 15.mp4). Don't add dialogue: the voices are added later and lip-synced. No deadline: make the clips today or another day, the episode waits for you (episodes never skip). Tip: Flow's free tier is often paused ~14:00-17:00 UTC.
