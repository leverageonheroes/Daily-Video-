# Ask The Elders - 30 episodes (8 clips x 8 s = 64 s)

## ELD-001 - What 80-year-olds wish they'd done with money
*Hook:* Money advice from 80-year-olds

1. **Hook:** I'm eighty-four. I earned good money my whole life. Here's what I'd do differently.
2. **Lesson:** Buy time, not things. Every gadget I bought is in a landfill. My trips are still in me.
3. **Lesson:** Pay yourself first. Ten percent of every pay packet, before anyone else touches it.
4. **Lesson:** Only lend money you're happy to call a gift. I lost two friends learning that.
5. **Lesson:** Don't buy a car to impress anyone. Nobody remembers your car. They remember if you showed up.
6. **Lesson:** Learn how money works before you earn much of it. Nobody else will teach you.
7. **Lesson:** Talk about money with your partner early. Silence about money ended more marriages than I can count.
8. **Closer:** And don't save all your happiness for retirement. Half my friends never got there.

## ELD-002 - What a 60-year marriage teaches you about love
*Hook:* 60 years married. Listen.

1. **Hook:** I'm ninety-three. Married sixty-one years. Here's what nobody tells you about love.
2. **Lesson:** Love is a decision you make on the days you don't feel it.
3. **Lesson:** Never go to bed trying to win. Go to bed trying to understand.
4. **Lesson:** Marry someone who's kind to waiters. That's how they'll treat you in twenty years.
5. **Lesson:** Stay curious about them. The person you marry at twenty-five isn't the same at sixty.
6. **Lesson:** Hold hands in public, even when you're old and wrinkly. Especially then.
7. **Lesson:** Apologise first, even when you're only half wrong. Pride makes a very cold bed.
8. **Closer:** And say it out loud. I thought she knew. You never know how many days are left.

## ELD-003 - The regrets almost everyone has at 90
*Hook:* The regrets everyone shares

1. **Hook:** I'm eighty-one. I've watched a lot of friends say goodbye. They all regret the same things.
2. **Lesson:** I worried about what people thought. Those people never thought about me at all.
3. **Lesson:** I waited for the perfect moment. It never came. The ordinary moments were the good ones.
4. **Lesson:** I didn't call my brother for nine years, over something I can't even remember.
5. **Lesson:** I chose the safe job over the one I loved. Safe wasn't as safe as I thought.
6. **Lesson:** I never learned to swim. All these years, and I never once felt the ocean properly.
7. **Lesson:** I kept my best china for special occasions. Use it on a Tuesday. Tuesday is special.
8. **Closer:** If something scares you and excites you at the same time, that's your answer. Go.

## ELD-004 - Work advice from people who already retired
*Hook:* 45 years of work, in 40 seconds

1. **Hook:** I'm eighty-two. Forty-five years of work. Here's what actually mattered.
2. **Lesson:** Nobody at my funeral will mention my sales figures. Not one person.
3. **Lesson:** The company replaced me in two weeks. My kids couldn't replace the evenings I missed.
4. **Lesson:** Learn a skill people will pay for. Titles disappear. Skills don't.
5. **Lesson:** Be kind to the cleaner and the receptionist. They know everything, and they remember everything.
6. **Lesson:** Take every holiday you're given. Nobody gets a medal for unused leave.
7. **Lesson:** Change careers if you need to. I did at forty-five. Best decision of my working life.
8. **Closer:** Work hard, yes. But go home on time. The work will still be there. They might not.

## ELD-005 - What 90-year-olds wish they'd done for their body
*Hook:* Do this for your body now

1. **Hook:** I'm ninety-one. I'd trade every trophy I own for the knees I had at thirty.
2. **Lesson:** Walk every single day. Not for your weight. For your mind.
3. **Lesson:** Look after your teeth. Nobody warns you how much a smile costs at eighty.
4. **Lesson:** Sleep is not lazy. I wore my tiredness like a medal. It was a bill.
5. **Lesson:** Stretch every morning. Five minutes. Your back at seventy will thank you.
6. **Lesson:** Go to the doctor when something feels wrong. Brave and stubborn aren't the same thing.
7. **Lesson:** Cook your own food as often as you can. Then you know exactly what's in it.
8. **Closer:** Your body carries you your whole life. Stop treating it like a rental car.

## ELD-006 - Friendship lessons from the very old
*Hook:* What old friends teach you

2. **Lesson:** You don't need many friends. You need two who'll answer the phone at three in the morning.
3. **Lesson:** Friendships die from silence, not arguments. Send the text. Make the call.
4. **Lesson:** Keep some friends younger than you. Otherwise, one day, you'll have none left.
5. **Lesson:** Make new friends at every age. I made my best friend at seventy-three.
6. **Lesson:** Forgive your friends for small things. You'll need them to forgive you too.
7. **Lesson:** A real friend tells you the truth, gently. Keep those ones close.
8. **Closer:** Go to the reunion. Go to the wedding. Go. Showing up is the whole thing.
