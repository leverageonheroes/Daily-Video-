# The Crib Committee - 30 meetings (5 clips x 8 s = 40 s)

No names: every clip is a different baby. New babies, same complaints. The Chair always wears the tiny tie and reading glasses.

## CC-001 - Babies held a meeting about bedtime
*Hook:* The babies have notes on bedtime  
*Parenting note:* A predictable bedtime routine (bath, book, bed) helps young children settle and sleep better.

1. **Chair:** This meeting is in session. Agenda item one: bedtime. It's been chaotic.
2. **Sassy:** I wish bedtime looked the same every night. Bath, book, bed. Surprises are for birthdays.
3. **Wise:** I fight sleep because I'll miss you. Stay a minute longer. Then I'll let go.
4. **Dramatic:** You skipped three pages of the story last night! I noticed. I always notice!
5. **Closer:** Same routine every night. It's not boring to us. It's safe. Motion carried. (newborn: lets out a tiny loud burp)

## CC-002 - What babies wish you knew about tantrums
*Hook:* What a tantrum really means  
*Parenting note:* Toddlers can't calm big feelings alone yet. A calm adult nearby (co-regulation) and naming the feeling help most.

1. **Chair:** Order. Today we discuss what you call tantrums. We call them overwhelming.
2. **Dramatic:** I'm not giving you a hard time. I'm HAVING a hard time. Big difference!
3. **Sassy:** When you yell back, now there's two of us screaming. Who's the grown-up here?
4. **Wise:** Name the feeling for me. You're angry. You're sad. Hearing it makes it smaller.
5. **Closer:** Stay calm and stay close. Your calm becomes our calm. Motion carried. (newborn: hiccups once, loudly)
